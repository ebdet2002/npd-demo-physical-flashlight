# Fonts

## Display: Barlow Condensed (Google Fonts)
Industrial, slightly condensed grotesk used widely on tool/safety-equipment
packaging — reads as "rugged hardware" rather than "consumer gadget," fits
the warehouse/tradesperson brand positioning. Used for the product name,
headings, and the wordmark alongside the logo.

Fallback stack: `"Barlow Condensed", "Arial Narrow", Arial, sans-serif`

## Body / UI: Inter (Google Fonts)
Highly legible at small sizes on-device-adjacent UI (viewers, docs, spec
sheets), wide language/number support, free and ubiquitous.

Fallback stack: `Inter, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
